# Captures (raw)

The author's own material, recorded verbatim. **This file is upstream. Nothing in it is ever
published as canon.** Canon shows the masked figure only.

Captures are the **root set**. Together with the author's laws, they are the only thing an entry's
`derives_from` chain is allowed to terminate in. Give every capture a stable id, because entries
will cite it for years.

---

## Read this before asking the author anything

**These are seeds for story creation, not case files for investigation.**

When a capture withholds something (how it ended, who she was, what year it was, what it led to),
the withholding is **the room the story gets built in**, not missing evidence to recover. Do not
interview the author to complete a capture. Ask for material the bible does not have; never
interrogate material it does.

## Capture-first

Record it now, personalise it later. Fidelity and speed beat shaping. The literary pass (voice,
structure, masking decisions) is a separate, later job. The goal of this phase is **breadth and
faithfulness**: get them down while they are present, do not stop to polish.

## The standing rules

- **Verbatim.** Their words and their facts. Paraphrase at capture time destroys the datum
  permanently, and your register is not theirs.
- **Mask at build, not at capture.** Record real names upstream if the author gives them; the mask
  is applied when an entry is written. Flag the capture `mask-sensitive`.
- **Author redactions are standing.** Permanent, and never asked about again.
- **Never invent a motive.** Leave the blank. An author-supplied motive is authoritative and gets
  recorded **as theirs**.
- **Read for the seed, not the biography.** These are images supplied to drive invention. Capture
  faithfully and mask identities, without treating the material as a confession to tiptoe around.
- **Author blur is not memory blur.** When the author is vague, distinguish *he does not remember*
  from *he is choosing not to say*. They are different data and only one of them is a gap.

## The note on every capture

Three fields, always:

- **Could seed:** what this might become.
- **Mask-sensitive:** who or what needs masking, and how.
- **Do not invent:** the specific things a later pass must not fill in. This is the guard rail and
  it is the one that will be thanked.
